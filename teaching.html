In development

Open courses I am building to share freely. The goal is to make computational and data-driven tools in neuroscience and medicine accessible to trainees anywhere, especially in Latin America.

Mathematical statistics

Foundations for anyone who wants to read — not just use — modern methods: probability, estimation, hypothesis testing, likelihood, generalized linear models, and the asymptotic arguments that underpin most of applied biostatistics.

  • In preparation: lecture notes, problem sets, R and Python notebooks.

Computational neuroscience

From single neurons to networks. Dynamical systems, coding, plasticity, population geometry, and models of motor and cognitive function — with hands-on exercises in Python.

  • In preparation: lecture notes, problem sets, Python/JAX notebooks.

Machine learning and AI applied to health

Supervised and unsupervised learning, representation learning, and modern deep learning, framed around problems in clinical medicine. Emphasis on evaluation, bias, causal reasoning, and deployment realities in healthcare.

  • In preparation: lecture notes, case studies, Python notebooks.

Big datasets in medicine

Working with electronic health records, claims data, neuroimaging repositories, and large clinical registries. Study design, data engineering, reproducible pipelines, and the statistical pitfalls specific to observational data at scale.

  • In preparation: lecture notes, example pipelines, worked datasets.
